I am a M.Sc. student at [Southeast University (SEU)](https://www.seu.edu.cn/) under the supervision of Prof. Xiaosi Tan and Prof. Chuan Zhang, expected to graduate in June 2027.

My research focuses on the intersection of Generative Models, Bayesian Inference, and Variational Methods for solving high-dimensional inverse problems.
